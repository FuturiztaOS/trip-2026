🕐Day Plan
Arriving from Nice

📍 La Spezia
Evening
Drop bags · Walk Via Colombo (main street) down to harbour
Via Colombo: colourful steep street, limoncino shops, focaccia bakeries. The whole village is on this one street basically.
📍 Harbour📍 Via Colombo
Pre-dinner
🏰 Castle of Riomaggiore — free, 15min walk up from harbour
Best panoramic view of the village. Walk up Via del Castello. Sunset light is golden on the coloured buildings from here.
📍 Castle
Dinner
🍽️ Dau Cila — harbour-side, best seafood in Riomaggiore
Get outdoor harbour table (ask specifically). Order: Trofie al pesto (THE dish of Liguria) + Branzino al forno (sea bass) + house white wine. ~€35pp. Avoid the tourist trap places on Via Colombo.
📍 Dau Cila
After dinner
🍋 Bar Centrale — buy a bottle of local Limoncino to take home
~€8 a bottle. Made from local Ligurian lemons. Different from limoncello (smoother, less sweet). The one souvenir worth buying in Cinque Terre.
📍 Bar Centrale
🔍 Local Intel — Riomaggiore
🍽️ Eat & Drink
🌿
Trofie al pesto — short twisted pasta with fresh Ligurian basil pesto. Always handmade here. The pesto in Liguria is completely different from anything in Malaysia — no cream, no nuts, just basil, pine nuts, olive oil, Parmesan. Order it everywhere.
🐟
Fritto misto di mare — mixed fried seafood. Crispy, fresh, eaten with lemon. ~€14. Order as a starter.
🍷
Sciacchetrà wine — local sweet dessert wine made from semi-dried grapes. Ask for a glass after dinner. ~€8. Only made in Cinque Terre, can't get it elsewhere.
🧀
Farinata — thin chickpea flatbread baked in wood oven. Street food, ~€2 a slice. Grab at any bakery on Via Colombo.
🛍️ Buy
🍋
Limoncino liqueur — €8-12 a bottle. Smoother than limoncello. Only available locally. Bar Centrale has the best selection. Good gift.
🫙
Pesto in jars — €5-7. Buy "Antichi Sapori Liguri" brand if you see it — genuine local product. Last 1 year sealed.
💡 Tips
⚠️
Card not always accepted — carry €20-30 cash. Many small places are cash-only.
🚫
Don't swim at night — strong currents after dark at the harbour rocks.

🔍 Local Intel — Cinque Terre
🗺️ Village Guide
🟢
Vernazza — best harbour, best restaurants (Gambero Rosso), most photogenic piazza. Worth the most time.
🟢
Monterosso — biggest village, only beach, best for shopping (anchovies, pesto), most "normal" town feel.
🟡
Manarola — best viewpoint photo, Nessun Dorma sunset bar. Great for photos, less to do.
🔴
Corniglia — SKIP. 382 steps up from train station, no harbour, least interesting. Not worth it.

🛍️ Buy in Monterosso (Via Roma)
🐟

🌿
Pesto Genovese (jar) — €6-7. Antichi Sapori Liguri brand best. Keeps 1 year sealed.

💡 Tips
🎫

Free drinking water — look for "acqua potabile" fountains in each village. Refill your bottle, saves money.

📍 Chapel Bridge

🌉 Kapellbrücke (Chapel Bridge) — walk across it
Built 1333. Oldest covered wooden bridge in Europe. Look UP inside at the painted triangular panels — 17th century scenes depicting death and Lucerne history. Water Tower attached to bridge (used as treasury, then prison). Best photo: from the riverside looking at bridge + Water Tower together at dusk.
📍 Chapel Bridge
19:00
🦁 Lion Monument — 10min walk from bridge, free
Dying lion carved directly into a rock face. Commemorates Swiss guards killed defending Louis XVI in 1792. Mark Twain called it "the most mournful and moving piece of stone in the world." Takes 5min to see but you'll stand there longer. Very affecting.

🔍 Local Intel — Lucerne
🗺️ Must See in Lucerne
🌉
Kapellbrücke — walk it morning and evening. Different light, different mood. Look at the 67 painted panels on the ceiling — most are originals from 1600s.
🦁
Lion Monument — 10min walk from bridge. Free. 5 minutes but memorable. Don't skip it.
🏰
Musegg Wall — free walk along the medieval city wall with 9 towers. Great views over Old Town. 20min walk.
⛪
Jesuit Church — oldest Jesuit church in Switzerland, stunning pink baroque interior, free entry, 5min walk from bridge.
🍽️ Eat & Drink
🧀
Käsefondue — melted Gruyère + Emmental mixed with white wine. Bread dippers. CHF 25-30pp. If your bread falls in = buy everyone drinks (Swiss rule).
🥔
Raclette — big wheel of cheese melted under heat lamp, scraped over boiled potatoes + pickles. CHF 20-25pp. Very Swiss, very filling.
🐄
Zürcher Geschnetzeltes — veal strips in cream mushroom sauce over Rösti (potato cake). Classic Swiss German dish. CHF 28-35.
💡 Tips
🚂
Swiss trains run on time — to the minute. If your train says 08:10, be on the platform at 08:08. They close doors at 08:09.
💳
Cards accepted everywhere — Switzerland is very card-friendly. Tap pay works at almost everything including trains.
💰
CHF ≈ RM5 — Switzerland is expensive. Budget CHF 80-100pp per day (meals + activities). Everything costs ~5x Malaysia price.

🔍 Local Intel — Mount Titlis
🎿 Summit Activities
🌉
Titlis Cliff Walk — included in cable car ticket. Europe's highest suspension bridge at 3,041m. Free with your Rotair ticket. Non-negotiable — do this first thing.
🧊
Glacier Cave — included in ticket. Walk into the actual Titlis glacier. Ice is blue-green, carved tunnels, alien-looking. Temperature inside is cold even in summer — bring a layer.
🪑
Ice Flyer Chairlift — CHF 20 extra. Open chairlift over the glacier — you can look down into crevasses. 10min each way. Worth every franc.
🏔️
Snow Tubing — CHF 5 for a session. Slide down on inflatable tubes. Fun 20min activity if queue isn't long.
👗 What to Wear
🧥
Summit is −5°C in March — wear your warmest jacket + gloves. Down jacket mandatory. Jeans are fine for legs (it's not that cold on legs).
🕶️
Sunglasses essential — snow reflects UV. Without sunglasses, 2hrs at summit = sore eyes for 2 days.
🛍️ Buy at Summit
🍫
Swiss chocolate — Lindt + Toblerone are actually slightly cheaper at summit gift shops than at airports. Buy here. Lindor bags, Toblerone white chocolate.
🧀
Monastery cheese — buy at Engelberg monastery shop on the way back. Aged Alpkäse, €12-15 per piece. Excellent quality.

🏔️ The Eiger North Face
☠️
The "Murder Wall" — the Eiger's north face is 1,800m of near-vertical rock and ice. Called Mordwand (murder wall) in German. First successfully climbed in 1938 after many deaths. You'll see the exact face from Grindelwald village — it's directly above you.
📖
Read before you go — "The White Spider" by Heinrich Harrer — the classic account of the first ascent. Google "Eiger 1936 tragedy" for the full story.

🚌 Getting Around
🚢
Vaporetto (water bus) — €9.50 for 24hr pass, €16.50 for 48hr. Line 1 along Grand Canal is beautiful and worth taking once just for the views. Buy at any ACTV booth.
🚶
Walk everywhere — Venice is tiny. 30min walk crosses the whole island. Don't take water taxis (expensive, €15+) unless you have bags.
🗺️
Get lost on purpose — follow "Per Rialto" or "Per San Marco" signs when you need to reorient. Otherwise, wander the back canals.
🍽️ Eat Like a Local
🍷
Bacaro bars — traditional wine bars with cicchetti (small bites). Look for places with locals standing at the bar, not tourists sitting down. Rule: if there's a menu with photos, leave.
🐟
Sarde in saor — sweet-sour sardines with onion, pine nuts, raisins. Medieval Venetian dish. Unique flavour. Order it at every bacaro.
🍞
Baccalà mantecato — whipped salt cod on grilled bread. Creamy, mild. Best cicchetto in Venice.
🍹
Aperol Spritz — invented in Venice (or nearby). €3-5 at a bacaro standing bar. €12 at a tourist table on San Marco. Same drink, completely different experience.
⚠️ Tourist Traps to Avoid
❌
Restaurants with photos on the menu — always overpriced, never quality. Walk away.
❌
Sitting at San Marco cafes — €15 for a coffee with "music service charge." Standing at the bar = €1.50. Crazy difference.
❌
Water taxis unnecessarily — €15-25 minimum. Walk or vaporetto instead unless you have bags.

🖼️ Uffizi Room Guide
⭐
Rooms 10-14 (Botticelli) — Primavera + Birth of Venus. These are the rooms. Budget 20min just here. Everything else in the museum is secondary.
⭐
Room 35 (Michelangelo) — Tondo Doni — his only panel painting. Vivid colours, recently restored. 5min.
⭐
Room 83 (Leonardo) — Annunciation + Adoration of the Magi. 15min.
ℹ️

🗿 Accademia (Michelangelo's David)
📏
Scale is shocking — 5.17m tall. Photos don't convey it. Walk all the way around — he looks completely different from every angle. The back is as detailed as the front.
🗿
The Prisoners — 4 unfinished Michelangelo sculptures on the way in. Figures "emerging" from the stone. Arguable whether they're more powerful than David. 5min stop.
🥩 Bistecca alla Fiorentina Rules
🐄
Must be Chianina beef — specific breed from Val di Chiana. Ask the waiter to confirm. If they say "Angus" or can't tell you, it's not authentic.
🩸
Order it rare (al sangue) — they will literally refuse to serve it well-done at a proper restaurant. Trust them. The centre should be warm pink-red. This is the correct way.
⚖️
Priced by weight — €50-60 per kg. Order 1.2-1.5kg for two people. That's €60-90 for the steak alone. Worth every euro.
🛍️ Florence Shopping
👜
Genuine leather — Via della Vigna Nuova + surrounding streets. Family workshops. Don't buy from market stalls near Duomo (Chinese made).
🍦
Best gelato — Gelateria dei Neri (Via dei Neri) — pistachio + stracciatella. Always ask for a taste first. A good gelato shop won't pile it into a mountain shape — if it's piled high in a mound, it has filler in it.

🔍 Local Intel — Tuscany Drive
📸 The Famous Cypress Road Photo
🌲
Vitaleta Chapel (SP146) — the single most-photographed Tuscany image. Tiny chapel at the end of a cypress-lined dirt road. On SP146 between Pienza and San Quirico d'Orcia. Coordinates: 43.0834°N, 11.6573°E. Pull over — it's off the road. Morning mist makes it magical.
🌅
Best light — morning (soft mist) or golden hour (warm orange light on rolling hills). Try to be in Val d'Orcia between 9-11am.
🏘️ Pienza
🧀
Buy Pecorino di Pienza — sheep milk cheese, semi-aged or aged with truffle. Buy from any shop on main street. Small wheel ~€12-18. Best cheese of the trip. Wrap it well for travel.
🏰
Palazzo Piccolomini — €7pp. Built for Pope Pius II (who literally built Pienza from scratch as his ideal Renaissance city). Hanging garden with three-level loggia facing the valley. Stunning view from the garden terrace.
🍷 Montalcino Wine
🍾
Brunello di Montalcino — one of the world's great red wines. 100% Sangiovese Grosso. Minimum 5 years aging. Buy 1 bottle at the Fortezza enoteca — Brunello Annata €35-50, Riserva €80+. Cannot get this quality for this price anywhere else.
💡
More accessible option — Rosso di Montalcino = younger version, €15-20, excellent quality. Same grape, younger vine, more approachable. Buy this if budget is a concern.
🚗 Driving Tips
⚠️
ZTL zones — historic centres of Italian towns have ZTL (restricted traffic zones). Cameras fine tourists automatically. Do NOT drive into centro storico. Park outside and walk in.
⛽
Fuel — use highway service stations (Autogrill). Cheaper than village stations. Pay at pump with card.
🅿️
Parking in Rome — Hotel Artemide: ask about their parking arrangement. Or use NuovaPark near Termini (~€25/night). Drop car before checking in.

🏟️ Colosseum Tips
🎫
Book Arena Floor access — €5 extra at coopculture.it. Lets you stand on the actual arena floor where gladiators fought. Without it you're only on the outer ring. Worth it.
🌿
Roman Forum — included in same ticket. Find the Temple of Julius Caesar (small round temple in the Forum) — people still leave fresh flowers and offerings on it daily, 2,000 years later.
🏔️
Palatine Hill — also included. Higher up than Forum, best views looking DOWN at the ruins. Where Rome's emperors actually lived.
⛲ Trevi Fountain
🪙
The coin throw — RIGHT hand, throw OVER your LEFT shoulder (facing away from fountain). One coin = return to Rome. Two coins = find love. Three coins = marriage. The coins (~€1.5 million/year) go to a food charity.
⏰
Best time — mid-afternoon (14:30-15:30) is least crowded. Early morning (8am) is very photogenic but still has people. It's never empty.
⛪ Vatican
🆓
St Peter's Basilica is FREE — just queue. No booking needed. The scale inside is disorienting — everything is larger than it looks. The bronze baldachin (canopy) over the altar is 29m tall.
🏔️
Climb the dome — €8 walk / €10 elevator partway. Do the stairs — 551 steps, but the view from the drum (halfway) over the Sistine Chapel roof is unique. From the top: all of Rome.
💂
Swiss Guards — must be Swiss citizens, Catholic, single men aged 19-30. Their striped uniform was designed by Michelangelo (disputed, but the story is good). They are genuinely trained soldiers.
🍽️ Last Dinner — Testaccio
🍝
The holy trinity of Roman pasta — Cacio e pepe (cheese + pepper, no cream), Carbonara (egg + guanciale/pork cheek, no cream), Amatriciana (tomato + guanciale). Order all three and share. These are the three dishes Rome invented.
🍕
Roman pizza — thin and crispy (NOT like Naples). Baked in electric oven. Da Remo in Testaccio = the best Roman pizza. Order the simple ones: Margherita, bianca (white, no tomato).
🍻
Supplì — Roman fried rice balls with tomato + mozzarella. €1.50-2 each. Street food eaten standing. Order as starter everywhere.
